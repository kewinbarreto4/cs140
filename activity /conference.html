General Conference Talks

Conference Talk 1

About the Talk

This talk teaches about having faith in Jesus Christ and trusting Him during different situations in life. The speaker explains that we can become stronger when we turn to the Lord and try to follow His teachings. The message also reminds us that the gospel can help us find peace and direction.

My Thoughts

I liked this talk because it reminded me that I should trust Jesus Christ more, especially when things do not go the way I expect. Sometimes I worry about the future, but this talk helped me remember that I do not have to figure everything out by myself. I want to have more faith and continue trying to follow Christ in my daily life.

Conference Talk 2

About the Talk

This talk focuses on having courage and faith when we face challenges. The message teaches that difficult experiences are part of life, but we can receive strength and help from Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ. We should not allow fear to stop us from doing what is right.

My Thoughts

This talk made me think about some of the challenges I have experienced in my own life. Moving away from home and being in a new environment can sometimes feel difficult. I liked the reminder that I can pray and trust God when I feel unsure. I want to remember this message when I face something that makes me afraid or uncomfortable.

Conference Talk 3

About the Talk

This talk teaches about making good choices and becoming wiser in the way we live. The speaker reminds us that our decisions are important and that we should think about the consequences of our choices. Following the guidance of the Lord can help us make better decisions and become better people.

My Thoughts

I thought this was a good reminder that small choices can make a big difference over time. Sometimes it is easy to make a decision without thinking about what will happen later. This talk encouraged me to slow down, think carefully, and choose things that will help me grow spiritually and personally.

My Overall Thoughts

All three talks gave me different things to think about. One thing I noticed is that faith, courage, and good choices are connected. When I have faith in Jesus Christ, I can have more courage to face challenges and make better decisions. These messages reminded me that I can continue to grow and become a better disciple of Jesus Christ.
